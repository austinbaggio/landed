▎ Privacy Policy — Landed – All-In Auction Pricing
▎ Last updated: July 24, 2026
▎
▎ Landed does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or browsing data. It does not use analytics, trackers, cookies, or advertising.
▎
▎ The extension reads the listed price on supported auction pages (Bring a Trailer, Cars & Bids, Car & Classic) solely to calculate and display an estimated all-in cost in Canadian dollars. This processing happens entirely on your device; page content is never sent anywhere.
▎
▎ Your settings (province and cost preferences) are stored locally in your browser via Chrome's storage API and are synced only through your own Google account, if you have Chrome sync enabled. The developer has no access to them.
▎
▎ The extension's only network request is to a public exchange-rate service (open.er-api.com) to retrieve current currency rates. This request contains no personal or identifying information beyond the standard network metadata sent by any web request.
▎
▎ No user data is sold or shared with third parties. Questions: [your email].
▎
▎ Changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
